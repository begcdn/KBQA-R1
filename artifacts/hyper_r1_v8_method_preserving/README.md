# HyPER-R1 v8 SFT Readiness Bundle

This bundle freezes the evidence from the full GrailQA demonstration build at
commit `b474ad2` on branch `codex/hyper-r1`.

## Method intent

HyPER-R1 is an executable graph-search policy that keeps several relation
hypotheses active instead of committing to one path immediately. The policy can
open and widen relation frontiers, select a hypothesis for continuation, prune
only after visible contradictory evidence, combine branches, apply logical
operators, and commit when one hypothesis covers the complete question.

The SFT corpus is intended to teach that policy, especially two behaviors that
ordinary single-path demonstrations do not teach:

1. preserve plausible alternatives when the highest-ranked relation is wrong;
2. recover later by selecting a lower-ranked branch or requesting another page.

Gold programs choose the verified teacher action. They are not inserted into
the relation proposal set. Candidate relations are generated by the same
question-and-state-conditioned ranker used at inference, and every saved
trajectory is replayed through the runtime action contract.

## Run configuration

- Source: complete processed GrailQA training split, 43,851 questions
- Relation model: `princeton-nlp/sup-simcse-roberta-large`
- Relation page size: 6
- Active hypothesis limit: 24
- Executed-node limit: 24
- Turn limit: 16
- Graph-query workers: 8
- Runtime substrate: MID-keyed Freebase in Virtuoso
- Training/validation split: question-disjoint SHA-256 split, 95/5

## Main results

- 37,862 verified trajectories qualified before balancing and exact deduplication.
- 29,774 demonstrations were accepted after curriculum selection.
- 28,312 are in the training split.
- 140,731 distinct training decision states contain zero contradictory teacher actions.
- Every saved trajectory replays successfully.
- Exact duplicate trajectory rate is zero.
- Entity display-label coverage is 99.77%.
- Gold relation is top-1 only 49.88% of the time.
- Gold relation appears on the first frontier 91.62% of the time.
- Paging recovers another 3.63%, reaching 95.25% within the node budget.
- Both required conjunction branches are proposed on the first frontier in 99.69% of cases.
- Continuation relation first-frontier recall is 90.22%; continuation top-1 is 37.45%.

Recovery therefore has 1,655 verified trajectories. The builder's readiness
gate requires `max(500, 10% of multi-hop trajectories)`. With 26,354 multi-hop
trajectories, that threshold is 2,636, so `structurally_ready_for_sft` is false.
All other readiness checks pass. This threshold is a curriculum heuristic, not
a replay-validity or inference-compatibility failure.

The observed recovery funnel is 1,957 detected opportunities -> 1,737 verified
demonstrations built (88.8%) -> 1,655 retained in the training split (95.3% of
built, 84.6% of detected). Thus, the final split and ordinary filtering account
for little of the low count. The unresolved question is earlier: whether 1,957
is the natural base rate in GrailQA or an underestimate caused by the recovery
opportunity definition, proposal visibility, budgets, or teacher construction.
That cause must be established before oversampling or generating more examples.

## Important limitations

- The teacher uses gold programs to select the correct action among naturally
  proposed executable actions. This is expected supervision, but the reviewer
  should check that no private gold feature is visible in the student state.
- GrailQA naturally contains far more direct progress than explicit recovery.
  Training without balancing may underteach the method's distinctive behavior.
- Three-hop acceptance is 37.36%; four-hop acceptance is 5%. The corpus may
  therefore underrepresent difficult long-horizon behavior despite passing its
  current deep-trajectory threshold (383 present versus 134 required).
- The runtime ranker still misses some required relations: first-frontier recall
  is not 100%, and 71 demonstrations were excluded because a root source could
  not be derived publicly from the question.
- The large train files remain on the RunPod volume and are intentionally not in
  Git. `stratified_samples.jsonl` contains deterministic compact examples from
  all nine trajectory families, with extra recovery examples.
